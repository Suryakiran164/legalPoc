================================================================================
LEGAL METROLOGY PACKAGED COMMODITIES COMPLIANCE POC
Sample Workflow and Edge Cases
================================================================================

PART A - HAPPY PATH

Scenario:
A field inspector uploads a photo of a packaged food product and reviews the
results.

Step 1: Register and log in
- User signs up with email and password.
- Backend creates a row in the Supabase users table.
- JWT is returned to the client and stored in localStorage.

Step 2: Upload package image
- User goes to the New Scan page and uploads a JPEG, PNG, or WEBP image.
- The request is sent to POST /api/scans.

Step 3: Backend receives the image
- The file is stored in Supabase Storage.
- The backend calls the OCR microservice.

Step 4: OCR extraction
- OCR reads text such as manufacturer name, commodity name, MRP, net weight,
  dates, and consumer care details.
- Raw OCR output plus confidence is saved in the scan record.

Step 5: Structured field extraction
- The backend parses OCR text into fields like:
  manufacturer_name
  commodity_name
  net_quantity_value
  net_quantity_unit
  mrp_raw
  mfg_date
  best_before_date
  consumer_care_phone
  consumer_care_email

Step 6: Rule engine evaluation
- The backend loads active rules from the Supabase rules table.
- Each rule checks required declarations and returns an observation status.

Example statuses:
- ok
- possible_issue
- missing_mandatory_declaration
- requires_human_verification

Step 7: Human review
- Inspector verifies the extracted fields on the scan detail page.
- If a field is wrong, they correct it and save.
- Saving corrections re-runs the rule engine and updates the scan.

Step 8: Final review decision
- Inspector records a conclusion like:
  "Cleared"
  "Escalated for physical inspection"
  "Needs re-labelling check"
- Decision is stored with notes and timestamp.

Step 9: PDF report
- The inspector can download a fresh PDF from the scan page.
- Report contains the three sections: OCR data, AI observations, and human
  review decision.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART B - EDGE CASES

1. Blurry or low-quality image
- OCR confidence may drop.
- Low-confidence outcomes are escalated to requires_human_verification.
- Inspector should re-take the photo or manually verify the fields.

2. No readable text in the image
- The backend may detect missing mandatory declarations across many rules.
- This should be treated as an upload or capture issue rather than a legal
  conclusion.

3. Imported product
- If the label contains imported product indicators, the country-of-origin
  check is evaluated.
- If the field is absent, the rule is reported as missing.

4. Best before or use-by rules
- Some products may not require a best-before declaration.
- The rule engine handles this based on the extracted field state and the
  configured rules.

5. Dates in unusual formats
- The extraction logic attempts to parse common date formats.
- Unclear dates often trigger human verification instead of a wrong rule
  result.

6. OCR split text or formatting issues
- The split between label lines may cause a field to be read incorrectly.
- This is why the human correction flow exists.

The important principle is:
AI observations help the human reviewer, but the human review decision is the
final authority.
